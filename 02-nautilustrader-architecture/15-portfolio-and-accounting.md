# 15 — Portfolio and accounting

[Course home](../README.md) · [All lessons](../COURSE.md) · [Section index](README.md) · [← Previous](14-risk-engine.md) · [Next →](15a-accounts.md)

**Learning objective:** Reconcile cash, inventory value, and net equity.

The Portfolio answers higher-level questions than an individual position.

Examples:

- What positions exist?
- What's my exposure?
- What's my realized PnL?
- What's my unrealized PnL?
- What's my total equity?

Think hierarchy:

1. Orders
2. Fills
3. Positions
4. Portfolio

## Practical clarification

Keep account balance and equity concepts separate. For the simple unleveraged, single-currency example, equity equals cash plus marked inventory. Trade PnL, fees, dividends, external deposits, and currency conversion require their own accounting treatment.

## Practice

Start with 10,000 cash. Buy 10 shares at 100 with a fee of 1; mark them at 102. Find cash and equity.
