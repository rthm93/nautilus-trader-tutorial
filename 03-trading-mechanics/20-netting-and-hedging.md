# 20 — NETTING versus HEDGING OMS

[Course home](../README.md) · [All lessons](../COURSE.md) · [Section index](README.md) · [← Previous](19-positions.md) · [Next →](21-position-sizing.md)

**Learning objective:** Separate internal position attribution from the broker account position.

For equities, you will probably want to understand **NETTING** first.

NETTING roughly means:

- BUY 100
- SELL 30
- Position = LONG 70

HEDGING allows multiple internal positions to remain distinct.

For your initial long-only equity strategy:

> Use a net-position mental model.

Don't introduce hedging-mode complexity without a reason.

## Practice

Two strategies hold +100 and -30 internally. What economic account exposure must you reconcile?

## Check your understanding

Net +70 for that instrument, while retaining each strategy’s attribution. OMS settings do not grant broker permission to short or hedge.
