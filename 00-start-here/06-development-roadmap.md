# Development roadmap

[Course home](../README.md) · [All lessons](../COURSE.md) · [Section index](README.md) · [← Previous](05-learning-sequence.md) · [Next →](07-topics-to-postpone.md)

Divide the journey into four milestones.

## Milestone 1 — "I understand Nautilus"

You can explain:

- Strategy
- Actor
- Instrument
- DataEngine
- Cache
- MessageBus
- RiskEngine
- ExecutionEngine
- Portfolio
- Order
- Fill
- Position
- BacktestNode
- TradingNode

without looking them up.

## Milestone 2 — "I can build a valid backtest"

Your strategy includes:

- realistic data
- correct timestamps
- no look-ahead
- fees
- slippage assumptions
- position sizing
- risk limits
- out-of-sample testing

## Milestone 3 — "I can operate a trading system"

It handles:

- restarts
- rejections
- partial fills
- disconnects
- stale data
- reconciliation
- logging
- alerts
- kill switches

## Milestone 4 — "I can research strategies"

Only here should most of your attention shift toward discovering alpha.

That ordering may feel backwards.

It isn't.
