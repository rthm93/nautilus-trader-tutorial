# 39 — Short selling

[Course home](../README.md) · [All lessons](../COURSE.md) · [Section index](README.md) · [← Previous](38-halts.md) · [Next →](40-liquidity-and-volume.md)

**Learning objective:** Identify additional borrow, margin, and loss constraints.

Do not use short selling for your first strategy.

It adds concepts including:

- borrow availability
- borrow cost
- hard-to-borrow securities
- recalls
- short-sale restrictions
- margin
- unlimited theoretical loss

Start long-only.

Add shorting only after the rest of the system is boringly reliable.

## Practice

You sell short at 100 and the price rises to 250. What is loss per share before costs?

## Check your understanding

150. The price increase is not capped at your original proceeds; borrow availability and forced buy-ins create additional risks.
